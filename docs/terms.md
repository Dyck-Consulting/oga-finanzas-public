You may import CSV and PDF files you have a right to use. You are responsible
for ensuring you are authorised to use any file you import, including statements
belonging to other members of your household.

Where a statement includes a total printed by the bank, the App compares that
total against the sum of the parsed items and blocks the import when they do
not match. This is a safeguard, not a guarantee: it cannot detect an error that
preserves the total, and it cannot verify a statement that carries no printed
total.
