# Privacy policy — Oga

**Last updated: 17 November 2026**

Oga is a personal finance app for households, published by Dyck Consulting.
This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: **Oga does not collect anything.** There is no account, no
server, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

---

## 1. Who we are

Oga is published by Dyck Consulting (Asunción, Paraguay). You can reach us
at the contact email listed on the app's Google Play page.

## 2. What we collect

**Nothing.**

Oga has no user accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no
advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not know
who you are, that you installed the app, or how you use it.

## 3. Where your data lives

Everything you enter or import lives in a database file on your own device:

- **Accounts and credit cards** you create — name, institution, and the closing
  and due days you configure.
- **Transactions** imported from bank statements or entered by hand — amount,
  date, description, category, and the household member you assign it to.
- **Category rules** you create or modify.

This data is stored by the app on your phone and is never transmitted anywhere.
There is no sync, no cloud backup, and no server we could read it from.

## 4. Importing bank statements

To import a statement, you choose a CSV or PDF file through your phone's file
picker. The file is read and parsed **entirely on your device**, including the
PDF parsing, which runs off the main thread but still locally. The file is not
uploaded, and Oga does not ask for a bank login or connect to your bank.

## 5. Permissions the app requests

Oga requests no network permission. It cannot send or receive data over the
internet, even if it wanted to.

The only system interaction is the file picker used when you choose a statement
to import, which hands the app the one file you selected.

## 6. Sharing and disclosure

We cannot sell, share, or disclose your data, because we never receive it. No
third parties have access to your financial information through us.

## 7. Deleting your data

Your data is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear its storage
through your phone's settings. Because we never held a copy, there is nothing
we could delete on our side and no deletion request to file.

If you want to remove individual records, the app lets you delete accounts,
cards and transactions from within it.

## 8. Children

Oga is not directed to children. It handles personal financial information and
is intended for adults managing a household budget.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Oga changes how it handles data — for example, if
optional cloud backup is ever added — this page will be updated before that
version ships, and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also
be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact email on the app's
Google Play page.
